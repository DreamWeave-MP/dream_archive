Readers for the BSA and BA2 archive formats, as a Jess library.

<!-- more -->

A [Jess](https://github.com/DreamWeave-MP/jess) package, `org.dreamweave.dream-archive`. Add it to a `jess.toml` and
`jess workspace sync` pins it from this site:

```toml
[dependencies]
"org.dreamweave.dream-archive" = "0.1"
```

Every release below is the package's own archive, with its content digest beside the archive's
SHA-256; `jess.lock` pins the first and checks both.
